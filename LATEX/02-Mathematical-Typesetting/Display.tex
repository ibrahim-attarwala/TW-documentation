\section*{Inline vs Display Math}
Inline equations are written within single dollar signs or parentheses, such as $x + y = 10$. 
Display equations are centered on their own lines using brackets or environments:
\[
E = mc^2
\]

\section*{Mathematical Fonts and Symbols}
Different typefaces can be applied to mathematical text:
\begin{align*}
\text{Calligraphic:} & \quad \mathcal{RQSZ} \\
\text{Fraktur:} & \quad \mathfrak{RQSZ} \\
\text{Blackboard Bold:} & \quad \mathbb{R}, \mathbb{Q}
\end{align*}

Greek letters and logic symbols include $\alpha, \beta, \gamma, \pi, \theta$, along with set operations like $A \cap B$ and $A \cup B$.

\section*{Calculus and Core Structures}
Fractions, roots, and calculus operators are structured as follows:
\[
\sum_{i=1}^{n} i = \frac{n(n+1)}{2}, \quad \int_0^\infty e^{-x} \, dx = 1, \quad \sqrt[3]{y}
\]
